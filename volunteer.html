Volunteer Opportunities

📚

Teaching & Mentoring

Support students through tutoring, mentoring, and teaching sessions. Help with academic support and career guidance.

  • Subject expertise (Math, Science, English, etc.)
  • Patience and communication skills
  • Commitment: 2-4 hours per week
🎯

Event Support

Help organize and manage events, workshops, and community programs. Coordinate activities and ensure smooth execution.

  • Event planning and coordination
  • Logistics and management
  • Commitment: Event-based
💰

Fundraising

Assist in fundraising activities, donor outreach, and resource mobilization. Help us reach more supporters.

  • Communication and networking
  • Creative fundraising ideas
  • Commitment: Flexible
💼

Administrative Support

Support office operations, data management, documentation, and coordination tasks.

  • Organizational skills
  • Basic computer skills
  • Commitment: Flexible hours
🚑

Disaster Relief

Be part of our rapid response team during emergencies. Help with relief distribution and field work.

  • Physical fitness
  • Ability to work in challenging conditions
  • Commitment: On-call basis
📸

Media & Content

Help with photography, videography, social media, and content creation to document our work.

  • Photography/videography skills
  • Social media management
  • Commitment: Flexible
