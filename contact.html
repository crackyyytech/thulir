Make a Donation

Your contribution makes a real difference

Your donation helps us continue our mission of empowering communities through education, social welfare, and service. Every contribution, no matter the size, makes a meaningful impact.

Ways to Donate

  • Online Donation: Use our secure online payment gateway
  • Bank Transfer: Direct transfer to our trust account
  • Cheque/DD: Send to our registered office address
  • In-Kind Donations: Educational materials, books, supplies

All donations are eligible for tax deduction under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act. We provide donation receipts for all contributions.

Become a Volunteer

Join our team of dedicated volunteers

Volunteers are the heart of our organization. With over 1000 trust members, we have a vibrant community of individuals committed to making a difference. Join us and be part of something meaningful.

Volunteer Opportunities

  • Education Support: Tutoring, mentoring, and teaching
  • Field Work: Community visits and assessment
  • Event Management: Organizing programs and events
  • Administrative Support: Office work and coordination
  • Disaster Relief: Emergency response and relief work
  • Fundraising: Helping raise resources for programs

We welcome volunteers of all ages and backgrounds. Whether you can commit a few hours a week or participate in specific events, your time and skills are valuable to us.
