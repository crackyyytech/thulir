About Our Trust

Empowering communities through education, compassion, and service

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To create a society where every individual, regardless of their socio-economic background, has access to quality education, opportunities for growth, and the support needed to realize their full potential. We envision a world where compassion and service are the cornerstones of community development.

Our Mission

To empower the less fortunate through comprehensive educational programs, skill development initiatives, and compassionate social welfare services. We are committed to:

  • Providing quality education and learning resources to underprivileged children
  • Developing life skills and values through traditional and modern teaching methods
  • Responding to community needs through timely social welfare and disaster relief programs
  • Ensuring no one in our community goes hungry through our food initiatives
  • Building a network of volunteers and supporters committed to social change

History & Values

Sathya Unar Charitable Trust was established with a deep-rooted commitment to serve society and make a meaningful difference in the lives of those who need it most. Our journey began with a simple yet powerful belief: that education and compassion can transform communities.

Over the years, we have grown from a small initiative to a trusted organization with over 1000 members, benefiting more than 200 students, and conducting over 250 field visits. Our work spans across various domains including education, skill development, disaster relief, and community welfare.

Our Core Values

Compassion

We approach every individual and situation with empathy, understanding, and genuine care for their well-being.

Integrity

We maintain the highest standards of honesty, transparency, and ethical conduct in all our operations.

Excellence

We strive for excellence in all our programs, ensuring quality education and impactful social services.

Service

We are dedicated to selfless service, putting the needs of the community above our own interests.

Inclusivity

We believe in creating opportunities for everyone, regardless of their background, gender, or circumstances.

Innovation

We continuously seek innovative solutions to address social challenges and improve our impact.

Founder's Message

"True happiness comes from giving back to society and making a positive impact in the lives of those who need it most."

Dear Friends and Well-wishers,

It is with immense gratitude and humility that I welcome you to Sathya Unar Charitable Trust. Our organization was born from a simple yet profound realization: that each of us has the power to make a difference, and that together, we can create lasting change in our communities.

Education has always been close to my heart. I believe it is the most powerful tool we have to break the cycle of poverty, empower individuals, and build stronger communities. However, I also recognize that education alone is not enough. We need to address the holistic needs of individuals and communities – from basic necessities like food and shelter to skill development and emotional support.

Over the years, I have witnessed firsthand the transformative power of compassion and service. Whether it's seeing a child's eyes light up when they understand a new concept, watching communities come together during times of crisis, or observing the growth of our volunteers as they serve others – these moments remind me why we do what we do.

Our journey has not been without challenges, but every obstacle has only strengthened our resolve. From responding to natural disasters like the Gaja cyclone and Kerala floods to adapting our programs during the COVID-19 pandemic, we have learned to be resilient, innovative, and always focused on our mission.

As we look to the future, I am filled with hope and determination. We have ambitious plans to expand our reach, deepen our impact, and touch more lives. But we cannot do this alone. We need partners, volunteers, donors, and well-wishers who share our vision of a more equitable and compassionate world.

I invite you to join us in this journey. Whether you choose to volunteer your time, contribute financially, or simply spread the word about our work, your support makes a difference. Together, we can empower the less fortunate, transform communities, and create a brighter future for all.

Thank you for being part of our mission.

With gratitude and hope,
Founder, Sathya Unar Charitable Trust

Our Team

The Sathya Unar Charitable Trust is led by a dedicated team of trustees and volunteers who are committed to our mission. Our team brings together diverse expertise in education, social work, administration, and community development.

We are grateful to have over 1000 trust members who contribute their time, skills, and resources to make our programs successful. Our volunteers are the backbone of our organization, working tirelessly in the field to ensure that our services reach those who need them most.

If you are interested in joining our team as a trustee or volunteer, please contact us. We welcome individuals who share our passion for service and social change.
